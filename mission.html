Our Mission

Encryption is non-negotiable. Everything you send and receive is locked — even from us.

Every message, document, image, and file is encrypted client-side before it ever reaches our servers. NotepadAnon has zero ability to read your communications. In the event of a server breach, every byte of stored data is completely inaccessible — because we never hold the decryption key. Only the intended recipient can. The future of email is decentrilized.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

We cannot read your messages. By design.

Your content is encrypted on your device using keys derived from your identity before any transmission occurs. The server receives only ciphertext. The decryption key exists solely with the intended recipient. No NotepadAnon employee, no administrator, and no court order can extract the plaintext content of any communication from our servers — because the plaintext never arrives there.

  • Client-side encryption before any data leaves your device
  • Server stores only encrypted ciphertext — never plaintext
  • Decryption keys are held only by the recipient, never by us
  • A server breach exposes zero readable content to any attacker
  • NotepadAnon staff can never access your messages, files, or documents under any circumstance

Burn after read

Set any message to self-destruct the moment the recipient reads it. Once they navigate away, the message is permanently purged from the server. It cannot be recovered, forwarded, or subpoenaed — it simply no longer exists anywhere but in memory.

Timed destruction — 24, 48, 72 hours

Senders choose a hard destruction window: 24 hours, 48 hours, or 72 hours from send time. When the window closes, the message is automatically purged — regardless of whether it was read. Sensitive strategy, legal content, and operational communications cannot age into liability.

Automatic 30-day purge

Every message on NotepadAnon is automatically deleted after 30 days as an unconditional platform default. No persistent archive. No long-term data store for attackers, regulators, or discovery requests to access. All communications carry an expiration date — this is infrastructure policy.

Encrypted files and documents

PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, code files, images, and videos are encrypted identically to message text before leaving your device. The recipient is the only person who can open what was sent. All attachments are bound to the same burn rules as the message they travel with.
